You can use this website to contact your legislator.

1. Find Your Maryland Legislators

Start by finding your Maryland State Senator and three State Delegates using the official Maryland General Assembly website.

Find My Maryland Legislators

Enter your home address on the official website. Find your senator and three delegates, along with their official email addresses. Then return here.

2. Enter Your Information

Include your State Senator and all three State Delegates.

3. Write Your Representatives

Your email application will open with the message prepared. You can review and edit it before sending.
